# Discovery AI MVP Integrations And Future Roadmap

## 1. Correct MVP Boundary

Agents are MVP. External integrations are future.

The MVP includes:

- CrewAI agents, mock agent adapter and real agent adapter when available;
- discovery processing;
- structured outputs;
- human gates;
- manual evidence input;
- product and discovery UI.

The future scope includes:

- external integrations and automation connectors;
- conversational assistant;
- advanced dashboards;
- enterprise governance.

CrewAI agents must not be classified as future. They are the intelligence layer of Discovery AI and are part of the MVP product boundary.

## 2. MVP Agents And Processing Capabilities

These are part of MVP processing. Some may run through a mock adapter first, but they remain MVP capabilities.

- Strategy & Outcome Alignment;
- DOR Builder;
- Discovery Readiness;
- Existing Knowledge Review using manual/context input;
- Assumption & Risk Mapping;
- Methodology;
- Scope;
- Research Questions;
- Research Planner;
- Participant Strategy;
- Research Protocol;
- Evidence Processing;
- Synthesis;
- Evidence Triangulation;
- Insight QA;
- Opportunity Mapping / Opportunity Tree;
- Opportunity Prioritization;
- Solution Hypothesis;
- Validation Strategy;
- Strategic Recommendation;
- Delivery Handoff.

The MVP may simulate parts of this processing through fixture outputs, mock backend responses or local storage, but the product must still represent the agent-driven workflow as core behavior.

## 6. MVP Implementation Note

The MVP should validate the agent-driven discovery workflow with manual inputs and mock/fixture integrations before investing in enterprise connectors.

Do not move agents to future. Do not remove CrewAI from MVP. Do not describe agent processing as an integration.
